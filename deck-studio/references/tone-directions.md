# Three tone directions (Phase 3, the owner picks one)

Directions differ in **rhythm, density and imagery**, never in palette (the brand is fixed).

| | A: one line per slide | B: evidence on screen | C: story with artefacts |
|---|---|---|---|
| Rhythm | fast, 1.2 slides per minute, many `section` and `big-number` | slower, 0.8 per minute, `two-up`, `screenshot-zoom`, `code` | medium, chapters of 3 to 5, `image-full`, `quote`, `timeline` |
| Density | under 15 words per slide | up to 40 words plus one artefact | under 25 words plus one image |
| Imagery | type only, glow, numbers | screenshots, zooms, tables | photos, real documents, portraits |
| Steps | 0 to 1 per slide | 2 to 5 per slide | 1 to 3 per slide |
| Fits | keynotes, manifestos, closing talks | product demos, workshops, technical talks | founder stories, case studies |

Deliver one paragraph and one still per direction (the same slide, e.g. the key number, built three ways),
plus your recommendation in one sentence. The still is a real render of the kit, not a mock.
